Overview

Simple MP3 is a local music player. Your music and playlists stay on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

Data we collect

We do not operate a Simple MP3 account system and we do not sell personal data.

Data stored only on your device

  • Music library metadata indexed from files on your phone
  • Playlists, liked songs, recently played, and resume position
  • Optional Jellyfin server URL, username, and access token (on-device)
  • Downloaded offline audio and artwork (app private storage)

Data sent over the network

Only when you use Jellyfin Sync: your device talks to the Jellyfin server URL you provide. Credentials are used solely to authenticate and download media from that server. We do not receive your password or media on developer-owned servers.

No advertising or tracking SDKs are used for ads in the core app.

Android Auto

Media metadata is provided to the car through Android media session APIs for safe control while driving. This is handled by the Android/vehicle system, not uploaded to Simple MP3 servers.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes app-private data, including Jellyfin offline downloads stored in the app sandbox. Your system media files elsewhere on the device are not deleted.

Simple MP3 is a fast, beautiful local music player built for real listening — on your iPhone, iPad, and in the car with Apple CarPlay.

NO STREAMING ACCOUNT REQUIRED
Play the audio already on your device. Your library stays private on your phone.

PLAYLISTS THAT FEEL FAMILIAR
• Create and manage playlists with ease
• Liked Songs and Recently Played built in
• Add to playlist and queue from any track
• Same collections available when you connect CarPlay

APPLE CARPLAY READY
• Browse Playlists, Jellyfin Offline, Albums, Artists, Songs, and Recently Played from the dash
• Steering-wheel and CarPlay Now Playing controls
• Dark, high-contrast “car night” UI designed for night driving

JELLYFIN OFFLINE SYNC
• Connect to your Jellyfin server (LAN or manual URL)
• Download tracks for offline play
• Offline library works in the app and on CarPlay
• Optional Wi‑Fi-only downloads

MADE FOR THE ROAD
• Drive Mode — oversized controls and one-tap resume
• Resume last session (queue + position)
• Mini player + immersive Now Playing
• Auto Drive Mode when CarPlay connects (optional)

IMPORT & TOOLS
• Quick Connect — upload audio over your local Wi‑Fi from another device
• YouTube → Audio — look up titles and import files you already have (App Store–safe workflow)
• Search, library browser (songs, albums, artists, folders), shuffle & repeat

FAST & POLISHED
• Instant library from cache
• Deep violet night theme with electric teal accents
• Light theme available in Settings

PERMISSIONS
• Media Library — scan and play local music
• Network — Jellyfin sync and Quick Connect only (optional)
• Local network — discover servers and LAN uploads

Simple MP3 is free of streaming clutter. Your files. Your playlists. Your car.
